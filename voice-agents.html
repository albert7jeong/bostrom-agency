01 — What you get

A receptionist that never eats, sleeps or quits.

Not an answering service reading from a card, and not a phone tree. An agent trained on your business, working from scripts you approved — doing everything below on every single call.

  • Answers every call, 24/7 — first ring, no hold music, no "your call is important to us"
  • Books appointments directly — checks your real availability and confirms into Google, Outlook, Calendly or your booking system
  • Qualifies every caller — asks the questions you'd ask: what they need, when, where, budget — so you only call back people worth calling
  • Answers your FAQs in a natural voice — prices, hours, services, directions, trained on your business
  • Transcript & summary of every call — in your inbox before the caller has hung up their coat
  • Human handoff when it matters — transfers live to your cell for the calls that need you, takes a message when they don't
  • Your scripts, your rules — you approve every word it's allowed to say; it never improvises prices or promises
  • Screens the junk — telemarketers and robocalls handled politely and kept off your phone
02 — The math

Your voicemail is a goodbye.

The research on small-business phones is brutal: more than half of inbound calls go unanswered, and the vast majority of callers who reach voicemail never call back — they just dial the next result on Google.

You already paid for that ring. The ads, the SEO, the referrals — everything you spend on marketing exists to make the phone ring. The agent's only job is to make sure that money isn't wasted the second it does. One saved booking a month usually covers it.
