01 — What you get

One service. The complete pipeline.

Not software, not a course, not "consulting." We build and manage your entire outbound system — and everything below is included, always.

  • AI lead scoring & classification — every prospect screened by ICP fit, intent signals and company data before a single email goes out

  • Personalized campaign copy — written for you, A/B tested continuously; you approve every word before it sends
  • Full deliverability management — SPF, DKIM, DMARC, warmup and daily monitoring, so emails land in inboxes, not spam
